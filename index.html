01 / THE VISION

A different way
to see the world.

Knowledge rarely lives in isolation. A biological process connects to an ecosystem. A discovery changes our understanding of history. An observation opens a new line of inquiry.

01 / CONNECT
⟡

Beyond isolated facts

Navigate between people, places, systems, events, living things and ideas. Relations are explicit objects to inspect — not anonymous lines drawn between points.

02 / TRACE
◎

Evidence before certainty

Ask where a claim came from, what a source actually said, and how strong an interpretation is. Association is not automatically causation.

03 / REVISIT
↻

Understanding can evolve

New sources can change what we believe about earlier events. Keep the event, the evidence and the history of interpretation distinct.

02 / OUR APPROACH

From wonder
to understanding.

Our research brings together spatial exploration, structured provenance and time-aware interpretations. The experience should invite curiosity without hiding uncertainty.

03 / RESEARCH & RESPONSIBILITY

Curiosity,
with integrity.

Universe is an independent research and development project. Our public-facing knowledge dataset is not yet released, and the project is not presented as a definitive authority on any domain.

Current phase: research & prototypingSource verification and review workflows are under development.

Data principles

01

Provenance first. Preserve source identity, licensing, revisions and relevant context.

02

Explicit uncertainty. A source statement is not automatically an independently verified truth.

03

Human review. Candidate identities, interpretations and relationships require suitable review before publication.

04

Responsible access. Planned external research requests are identified, rate-limited and designed to respect provider policies.

i

External knowledge sources. We are evaluating open knowledge sources, including Wikidata, for future research. This site does not serve a live knowledge API or claim that its illustrative connections are verified factual assertions. Universe is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Wikimedia Foundation.
